Model comparison discipline

Compare the same valid time and comparable fields. Record initialization cycle, forecast hour, run age, and meaningful differences. Do not treat deterministic agreement as certainty; consult ensemble spread and observational trends.

Vertical analysis guide

500 mb

850 mb
